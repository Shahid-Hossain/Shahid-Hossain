## AI product prototypes

Each of these is a self contained product concept: a real problem, a written PRD, wireframes, an architecture flow, and an interactive demo you can click through. Metrics shown are targets, not results.

### CaseDeck
**Device repair and insurance claims operations**

An operations console for the device repair and claims lifecycle. Triages incoming cases, tracks each one through its stages, and surfaces where SLA risk is concentrating across new, refurbished, and replacement devices.

[shahid-casedeck.netlify.app](https://shahid-casedeck.netlify.app)

### TaskPilot
**Turns scattered messages into one ordered list**

Knowledge workers lose hours a week just reorienting between apps. TaskPilot reads email and chat, pulls out the requests people are actually making, and sorts them into Do today, This week, and No rush, with a plain reason attached to each one and an approval gate before anything is acted on.

[shahid-taskpilot.netlify.app](https://shahid-taskpilot.netlify.app)

### Verity 2.0
**Appeals and denial management for healthcare payers**

Extends Verity downstream into what happens after a denial. Builds the appeal packet, tracks deadlines, and identifies which denials are worth contesting.

[shahid-verity-2.netlify.app](https://shahid-verity-2.netlify.app)

### Verity
**AI prior authorization triage copilot**

Prior authorization decisions are slow, inconsistent, and expensive for everyone in the chain. Verity triages incoming requests, surfaces the clinical evidence a reviewer needs, and routes edge cases to a human instead of guessing.

[shahid-verity-prototype.netlify.app](https://shahid-verity-prototype.netlify.app)

### TripPilot
**AI travel planning agent**

Turns a loose intent such as ten days in Japan in spring into a day by day itinerary with real constraints on transit, opening hours, and pacing.

[shahid-trippilot-prototype.netlify.app](https://shahid-trippilot-prototype.netlify.app)

## Analytics and modelling

### ALDI Shopper Motivation Segmentation
**Factor analysis and multinomial logit in R**

Graduate capstone with ALDI through McCombs. Segments grocery shoppers by what actually drives store choice, then tests whether those segments can be identified from demographics alone. Two survey waves, both scored on frozen parameters so the year over year comparison holds.

Full pipeline, methodology writeup, and a synthetic dataset so the code runs for anyone who clones it.

[Repository](https://github.com/Shahid-Hossain/Capstone-Aldi-Shopper-Segmentation)
